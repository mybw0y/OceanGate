Who We Are

Precision at every port call.

OceanGate is Costa Rica's leading port agency and vessel support company. Founded to bridge the gap between international shipping standards and the operational realities of Costa Rican ports, we serve ship owners, charterers, and operators with the precision, responsiveness, and expertise their operations demand.

From the Caribbean terminals at Moín and Limón to the Pacific ports of Caldera, Puntarenas, Quepos, and Golfito — our teams are on the ground, around the clock.

Mission: To deliver world-class maritime agency services with local knowledge and global standards.
Vision: To be the most trusted name in Central American maritime operations.
