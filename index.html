Who We Are

Precision at every port call.

OceanGate is Costa Rica's leading port agency and vessel support company. Founded to bridge the gap between international shipping standards and the operational realities of Costa Rican ports, we serve ship owners, charterers, and operators with the precision, responsiveness, and expertise their operations demand.

From the Caribbean terminals at Moín and Limón to the Pacific ports of Caldera, Puntarenas, Quepos, and Golfito — our teams are on the ground, around the clock.

Mission: To deliver world-class maritime agency services with local knowledge and global standards.

    How It Works

    Port call process.
    Start to finish.

    01

    Pre-Arrival Coordination

    ETA notification received 48–72 hours out. We begin berthing allocation, authority notifications, and full service scheduling before the vessel reaches port limits.

    02

    Port Authority Communication

    Direct liaison with port captaincy, customs, immigration, and health authorities. All pre-arrival declarations submitted and clearances coordinated in advance.

    03

    On-Site Vessel Attendance

    A dedicated agent boards upon arrival, present throughout the call. Single point of contact for captain, officers, and all shore-side parties.

    04

    Cargo / Crew / Logistics Support

    Simultaneous management of cargo operations, crew changes, provisioning, spare parts delivery, and any technical or medical requirements during the port stay.

    05

    Departure Clearance

    All outbound documentation prepared: port clearance, customs exit, immigration stamps, and final manifest. Vessel departs without delays at the gate.

    06

    Final Reporting

    Full disbursement account, call report, and port cost summary delivered within 24 hours of departure. All documentation archived and available on request.
